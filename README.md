One line, under 200 characters, that someone could send to call in sick —
the funniest excuse in one breath, and nothing else. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36).

## The line

> I won't be in today. A mosquito got my eyelid at 3am and now my eye is swollen shut. Dina, my neighbour's kid, just asked who punched me.

137 characters. Half believable, fresh idea, and the laugh arrives on the
last word.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
node main.ts
```
